# Future Ideas

The following ideas are intentionally deferred; they are not part of the current application.

- Real-time co-authoring, comments, and editorial review workflows
- Semantic search and fully local embedding models
- DOCX and additional chat-provider archive importers
- Richer page-layout and word-processing controls
- Tagged accessible PDF generation and deeper EPUB validation profiles
- Premium multi-speaker audiobook production
- User-supplied local AI models and configurable AI providers
- AI-generated cover illustration, with explicit provider consent and provenance
- Custom bookshelf themes and reusable appearance presets
- Plugin or extension APIs
- Duplicate-book detection and merge assistance
- Automated backup restore drills and backup health reminders

Any networked feature should preserve Storybound's local-first default, require clear opt-in, and document exactly what leaves the device.
